One keyframe update, run over the mapping pass and then over the update pass. (1)~A posed keyframe gives RGB and, if available, observed depth; (2)~dense LSeg features, compressed by a PCA fit on the mapping frames (first three coordinates shown as RGB); (3)~one render and one backward pass~\cite{joseph2025gradbp} give every Gaussian its per-view sums; (4)~each view is one observation $\mathbf{u}_v$ with weight $\omega_v$; (5)~four running sums give (6)~the vMF posterior, the epistemic $u^{\mathrm{epi}}$ from the support $s$ and the aleatoric $u^{\mathrm{ale}}$ from the agreement $\hat\rho$ (grey: $s<\tau_{\min}$); (7)~a change test of all earlier evidence against the current view, with a threshold $\theta(h)$ from the static mapping pass; (8)~reset and depth-checked re-admission, which decide the robust set; (9)~a predictive render answers text queries. The index $n$ is dropped. Thumbnails come from validation scenes: Replica office\_0 (read-outs of the final map at the keyframe's pose) and, for (7) and $\theta(h)$, NeRF-Object-Removal scene 002, where orange marks the Gaussians that the test reset during the update, drawn over the photo taken after the chair was removed (inset: the map before the update).
